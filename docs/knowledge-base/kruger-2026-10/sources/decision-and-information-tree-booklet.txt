 TBCA | AIIM and Countries | Design proposal v1.0 | 11 October 2026

 One information bank. Two purposeful experiences. A uniform structure for 54 countries.

 AIIM helps an investor or trader find, locate and compare. Countries helps the same person understand the
 market, test entry or trade conditions, and choose the next practical step.

   AIIM                                                               Countries

   Where is it? What connects to it? Which location or                What does it mean in this country? What conditions apply?
   opportunity deserves attention?                                    What must I verify or arrange?

   Interactive map + question navigator + linked evidence panels      Country dossier + decision pathways + operating checklists

   Spatial patterns, routes, hotspots, layered comparisons            Country context, institutional roles, entry/trade requirements,
                                                                      available services


 This booklet includes the reusable master prompt and its executed design output, proper branching decision
 trees, information trees, placement tables, visual specifications and worked examples.

 Evidence basis: live TBCA pages, selected supplied mock-ups, nine populated country packs, the general catalogue and the two
 transaction blueprints. The companion JSON register maps the inspected schemas. This is a presentation design, not a published
 WebApp change or a certification of country facts.

 The recommendation
 Keep both tabs. Give AIIM the responsibility for discovery and evidence, and Countries the responsibility for
 country understanding and practical operating context. Countries also owns the commercial permission
 logic described in its blueprint. This adds a richer dossier to the current Countries page without recreating
 AIIM's analytical engine.

 Uniformity without monotony
 Use one chapter order, one field contract and one set of components for every country. Change the local
 content, administrative labels, relevant layers and data availability. Within that structure, alternate maps,
 compact tables, timelines, charts and question cards according to the decision being made.

 100% coverage means every permitted record is reachable in each tab. It does not mean every value must occupy the landing page or be
 drawn on the map. Non-geographic facts belong in map-associated panels in AIIM and in topic chapters in Countries.

 The Data Bank stores the evidence. AIIM maintains factual evidence and publication truth; Countries
 maintains country commercial truth. Each tab reads the same evidence and the relevant versioned commercial
 projection. Neither tab silently edits the other's authority. [S5: Part 19]

 Bind a fact to country ID, sector/asset/product ID, question ID, geography ID, source ID and version. Bind a
 lead to its template, evidence and location-precision class. Preserve every source field in a complete-record
 reader even when the summary uses only five of those fields.

 Suggested shared context: country_id, persona, sector_id, asset_or_HS_id, object_id, geography_id, layer/filter selection, observation
 period, language, display currency and source version. Historical source copies remain distinguishable from the current projection.

 A record can be repeated for a different decision
 A substation capacity record appears in AIIM as a node and a connection panel. In Countries it appears in the
 energy chapter as a constraint and in a grid-connection pathway. The record retains its source date, capacity
 units and warning. The new presentation adds a different question, not a different fact.

 Every branch includes a searchable table alternative. Users may browse as an investor, trader or both. Persona filters select relevance
 and default questions; they do not delete other information or confer commercial authority.

 Default view
 Start at Africa with a country selector and clear research-coverage states. After country selection, show a
 small relevant map stack. Investor preset: opportunities/leads, infrastructure, constraints. Trader preset:
 production or suppliers, storage/collection nodes, corridors/restrictions. All other layers remain reachable.

 The map occupies the analytical viewport. Promotional blocks move below it or into a first-visit guide. Retain TBCA's black/gold brand
 shell, but give data, warnings, selection and maps their own readable visual hierarchy. Proposed routes and components are not
 assertions that they already exist.

 I1-I8 restate the source blueprint's eight intelligence layers using an I prefix to avoid confusion with geographic
 depth. I9 corresponds to its governed observed-truth layer. [S5: Parts 19 and 21]

 Practical map stack
 Recommended default: one base thematic layer plus up to two overlays. Users can add more with a clutter
 warning and opacity controls. Reorder overlays, toggle them, inspect source/legend and save a stack. A
 disabled layer states whether the cause is missing data, withheld rights or lack of relevance.

 Lines and flows require evidenced paths and direction. Bubble area may encode sourced quantity; size must not silently switch to score. A
 choropleth requires matching period, units and geography. Missing data uses an explicit pattern, not the lowest-value colour.

 Precision is a first-class field
 In the ZA model, P4 means a seat-town point and P5 a district point. These represent locations for screening,
 not exact project sites. Show a hollow marker and “Approximate administrative location” at close zoom. Give
 verified site records a separate symbol and their actual precision. [S3: ZA_Municipalities, ZA_DataGaps
 DG-16]

 Information density changes with zoom
 At country level, cluster leads and show aggregates. At local level, show named records. At site level, show
 access and facility details only when sourced. Keep a “47 results, 12 visible in map area” style counter so the
 table and viewport are not mistaken for different datasets.

 Some source files use L0-L5 for country-to-site map levels, while the transaction blueprint uses L1-L8 for thematic intelligence. G and I are
 proposed display/implementation aliases that preserve both original labels in source metadata.

 Colours and figures
 Gold indicates selection and primary action. Use a sequential scale for quantity and a diverging scale for a
 defined positive/negative measure. Risk and service availability use labelled icons as well as colour. Reserve
 grey hatching for unavailable/unknown data. Do not let green imply verification and commercial activation
 simultaneously.

 Footnote contract
 Each figure/table states: title, measure and units, geography, observation period, source/record IDs, status,
 method/assumptions and limitations. Original commodity quotes include grade, location, currency, unit and
 delivery basis. Derived prices retain the FX source/date; they never appear as sourced quotations.

 Example footnote: “Source: ZA_Municipalities, record JHB, pack ZA-1.0 dated 9 Oct 2026. P4 seat-town point; not an exact project
 location. Boundary source vintage: 2016. See DG-01 and DG-16.” All icons require text labels and keyboard-accessible actions.

 D. Create one canonical information model and two presentation models. Every source field must have an

 E. Organise AIIM by persona, country, administrative geography, sector, asset/product, question, map layers

 F. Map IQ01-IQ45 and TQ01-TQ40 individually. Map all source-sheet families, country-specific extras, source
