## 1 \| A polite email does not make a project bankable

There is a familiar moment in project development when a sponsor announces that the venture is "investment ready" because a financial model exists and a compelling presentation has been printed. The technical drawings are preliminary, the off-take is a conversation, land rights are being clarified and the projected return has two decimal places. Evidently, accuracy in formatting has been mistaken for accuracy in the underlying business.

Bankability is not a compliment paid to ambition. It concerns whether a project can attract financing on acceptable terms because expected cash flows, enforceable rights, risk allocation, sponsor strength and completion arrangements support repayment and returns. A project can be socially valuable but not bankable under a proposed commercial structure. A project can be profitable in a model but impossible to finance because risks are unallocated. Those statements are not insults. They are diagnoses to be addressed.

The World Bank's PPP guidance describes bankability as the ability to attract both sponsor equity and required debt. It explains that lenders test whether operating cash flows can service debt with an acceptable margin and whether uncertainty remains within manageable bounds. That guidance concerns PPP and project-finance structures, not every business loan, but it captures the logic investors apply to long-lived infrastructure assets. \[1\]

A project developer's job is to convert a concept into evidence. That conversion costs money, time and discipline. Someone must commission the studies, confirm rights, negotiate commercial terms and resolve gaps. Asking a lender to fund all preparation without a credible structure is asking someone else to purchase the project's unknowns. Sometimes facilities support preparation; that still does not eliminate the need for sound appraisal.

## 2 \| The five things money cannot cure

Money cannot grant land rights that are legally unavailable. It cannot make customers sign an uneconomic agreement. It cannot substitute for an environmental authorisation. It cannot turn a technically impossible design into a feasible one. It cannot make a sponsor with no controls suddenly competent to manage complex procurement. Finance can enable a viable plan. It does not repeal physics, law or commercial arithmetic.

## 3 \| Demand must become contractable

For a road concession, energy plant, tower portfolio or industrial facility, projected usage and income require a clear commercial basis. Identify the party that pays, the legal basis of payment, volume and price risks, adjustment mechanisms, collection processes and remedies for default. Classify every off-take document honestly. An expression of interest, draft term sheet, negotiated framework and binding contract are different milestones. They deserve different probabilities in a lender's assessment.

Take a hypothetical industrial plant. A study forecasts demand for every unit produced, yet no buyers have agreed on quality specifications, delivery terms or penalties. The sponsor's model uses a confident sales curve. An investor asks who will buy output if market prices fall. The room answers that there is "strong sector interest". The project has not reached commercial readiness. Its presentation has merely reached creative maturity.

## 4 \| Allocate risk to parties able to manage it

A bankable project places construction, demand, operating, foreign-exchange, land, political, regulatory and counterparty risks where they can reasonably be managed, priced or mitigated. Insurance, guarantees, reserve accounts, performance securities and contractual relief can matter, but none is free. Shifting every risk to the weaker contracting party may make a spreadsheet appear safer while increasing the chance that the contract fails in practice.

Document who bears each material risk, the evidence supporting that allocation and the cash effect if the risk occurs. If construction costs rise, who contributes additional equity or contingency? If the buyer delays payment, how is debt service funded? If currency weakens, which revenues or hedges offset imported costs? If a public approval arrives late, which dates or obligations change? The answers must be enforceable, not optimistic.

The World Bank's discussion of preparing bankable infrastructure projects highlights feasibility work, procurement documents, social and environmental studies, risk protocols and market sounding with lenders. It is a practical reminder that bankability is assembled through preparation and informed structuring, not declared by the project sponsor. \[2\]

## 5 \| The model must explain the transaction

Build the financing model from physical and contractual drivers: production, yield, availability, tariffs, tenant activation, costs, maintenance, tax, working capital and capital expenditure. Trace those drivers to operating cash flow, debt service, reserves and distributions. Set sensitivities using defensible ranges. Verify that every model version agrees with the investment memorandum, construction budget and commercial evidence. A project IRR is a calculation, not a personality trait.

Distinguish the total project cost from the amount requested from one lender. Show committed sponsor equity, grants, mezzanine finance or other sources separately from possibilities. Historical spending may establish sponsor commitment or sunk development cost, but it cannot be used a second time to pay contractors unless actual cash remains available. A lender will test this. Please do it first.

## 6 \| Independent challenge is an asset

Commission competent technical, legal, commercial, insurance and environmental advisers where the stakes require. Use them to identify defects early, not simply to certify decisions already taken. Arrange a lender-style challenge session before formal credit committee review. Invite questions that may be uncomfortable: could the technology underperform, can the contractor actually deliver, is the land title sound, is the buyer creditworthy, what happens under downside debt-service cover?

Approvals should proceed through gates. At concept stage, establish basic viability. At feasibility stage, assess alternatives and economics. At structuring, align risks and contracts. Before close, reconcile financing commitments and conditions precedent. At construction, manage draws against independent progress evidence. At operations, report covenants and service performance. Every transition needs documentary proof.

Use a red-flag register and refuse to bury concerns under footnotes. An adverse permit finding that could stop construction belongs on the first page of the decision pack, not in annexure seventeen after the catering menu. Bad news becomes useful only when the decision-maker can still act.

## 7 \| The sponsor readiness scorecard

Score each area from zero to three: site control; regulatory approvals; technical design; independently tested capex; commercial demand; operating capability; financing structure; sponsor equity and guarantees; contract and procurement strategy; and governance. Zero means the evidence is absent. One means an outline exists. Two means material evidence exists but gaps remain. Three means checked evidence supports the proposed commitment. This is an internal diagnostic, not a recognised lender credit score.

A low score in a critical condition can override a high total. Nine excellent workstreams cannot compensate for an unavailable site. A credible sponsor knows the hard stops, assigns owners and budgets development work accordingly. A patient investor may tolerate visible uncertainty. Hidden uncertainty is far more damaging because it calls management judgement into question.

## 8 \| Final provocation

Bankability is earned through a sequence of difficult, boring, expensive and verifiable decisions. That is precisely why it separates a project from a project-shaped aspiration. If financing is repeatedly refused, investigate whether the issue is market conditions, lender mandate, pricing, capital structure or fundamental project readiness. Do not assume every refusal is a failure to recognise genius.

Stop asking banks to bless work that has not been done. Build a defensible case, acknowledge the risks and show exactly how debt and equity are protected. A charming email may secure an introduction. It cannot secure twenty-five years of cash flow. Evidence can.
